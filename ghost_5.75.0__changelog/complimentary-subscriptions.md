


Complimentary premium plans







































It's now possible to offer complimentary subscriptions to your members in a couple of clicks. This means you can give your colleagues, friends or family access to paid-member only content in Ghost, without them needing to sign up or enter any credit card details.

Simply navigate to the member profile you'd like to provide free access to, and toggle "Complimentary premium plan"
