


Date-based member filtering






































The member dashboard now has new and improved filtering options. Now it's possible to filter your members list by the date their member profile was created, the date their paid subscription started, or by their next billing date.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2022/03/date-based-member-filters-2.png)

These new filters allow you toquickly create segments and better understand your subscribers. For example, you can discover:

* How many new members were created in the past 30 days?
* How many new paid subscribers have I got since [date]?
* How many paid subscribers renew in the next 14 days?
