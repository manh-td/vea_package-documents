


More email design options






































We just added more new email design settings for your newsletters, including:

* Add a custom header image to all emails
* Customise the publication icon and title independently
* Pick a typography style and alignment for the header
* Disable post feature images in emails

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2021/07/email-design-new.png)

Email newsletters in Ghost are delivered with a beautiful HTML template that is optimized for popular email clients — all you need to do is pick your design settings and start sending.
