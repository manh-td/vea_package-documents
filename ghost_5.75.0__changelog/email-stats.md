


Email newsletter stats







































See how your email newsletters perform with our latest new feature: Email stats!

Now you can see the open rate for posts which are delivered as email newsletters at a glance inside Ghost Admin.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2021/01/email-stats-1.png)

Your posts automatically display how many people the email was delivered to and the percentage open rate.

Plus, Member profile pages now include some stats too, with an average open rate per member, number of emails received and a member activity feed.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2021/01/member-feed-2.png)
