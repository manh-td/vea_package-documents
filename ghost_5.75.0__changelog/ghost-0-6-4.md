


Ghost 0.6.4







































[Ghost 0.6.4](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/releases/tag/0.6.4?ref=ghost.org) is now available on GitHub, npm and Ghost.org. 0.6.4 contains a number of bug fixes, largely for features released in 0.6.3.

Highlights
----------

* **[Fixed]** Caching issues with previews
* **[Fixed]** Password protection not redirecting to blog
* **[Fixed]** Multiple owners after import
* **[Fixed]** Ghost logo linking to wrong URL
* **[Fixed]** Invite email missing contact email address

You can see the [full change log](https://gist.github.com/ErisDS/8df07196c345e469bc43?ref=ghost.org) for the full details of every change included in this release.

How to Upgrade
--------------

All Ghost(Pro) users are being **automatically updated** and will be running Ghost 0.6.4 shortly. You're welcome :)

For people running Ghost on their own servers, you can [download Ghost 0.6.4](https://ghost.org/docs/install/?ref=ghost.org) and then check out the [upgrade documentation](https://ghost.org/docs/update/?ref=ghost.org) over on our [support site](https://ghost.org/help/?ref=ghost.org). If you've missed out a version or two, don't worry you can upgrade directly from any 0.5.x version straight to 0.6.4.

Enjoy!
