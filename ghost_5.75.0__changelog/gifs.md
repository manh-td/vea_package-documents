


GIFs in Ghost






































It's the moment you've been waiting for — the Ghost editor now supports GIF cards. Type `/gif` to get started, then search for the perfect GIF for your post.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2021/12/GIFs-in-ghost.gif)

Click the GIF you'd like to add and it will exist in your post in all it's glory. GIFs also work in email newsletters 😎
