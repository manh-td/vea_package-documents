


Ghost 0.3 - Kerouac






































*After months of hard work, an unbelievable amount of caffeine, and a laundry list of enormous challenges - today I am incredibly proud to announce the release of Ghost 0.3 - Kerouac.*

This release represents a total of **975** commits across **843** issues from **20** incredible contributors all over the world. All in the last 4 months. We have a brand new, functioning, beautiful blogging platform. And it's built entirely in JavaScript. We've climbed an absolute mountain to get here, but this really is just the beginning.

What's in The Box
-----------------

*Ghost 0.3* comes with the full, gorgeous Markdown editor which so many of you have been excited about using. It has a stunning post management interface, it has a simple set of settings which allow you to configure your blog, and it comes with a clean and simple personal blogging theme called *Casper*.

Jack Kerouac
------------

**This first release of Ghost is named for [Jack Kerouac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Kerouac?ref=ghost.org), an American novelist and poet who, in many ways, inspired the Ghost editor.** Kerouac, when faced with the issue of repeatedly having to stop writing to change the sheet of paper in his typewriter, did something slightly different to the norm. He started writing on a 120 foot roll of teletype paper, because it allowed him to write continuously - without breaks. He subsequently wrote his entire book, *On The Road* in the space of 3 weeks.

The fascinating thing about this approach was not necessarily the efficiency which resulted, but rather the effect which it had on his writing style. Fast paced, spontaneous lyricism as a result of this format wasn't just a one-off. It went on to influence a generation of other writers who followed.

*The tools we use to write influence our creativity, and the Ghost editor is the 120 foot roll of teletype for the 21st century.*

It aims to allow you to type continuously, formatting on the fly, without clicking buttons or writing long HTML or switching tabs to preview your post. Just write.

Public Release
--------------

Today's release is for our 6,000+ Kickstarter backers. The people who believed in us before we even existed. Without them, none of this would be possible. This early release is the promised reward for their support.

**In a couple of weeks**, once we've ironed some bugs, smoothed some edges, and given those people a chance to try Ghost out... we'll be opening up Ghost to the public. The Github repository will go public, and everyone will be able to sign up for an account on Ghost.org.

The Beginning
-------------

This is really just the first milestone for Ghost. Thanks to the incredible success of the Kickstarter campaign, we are not done now. We're working on Ghost full time, and we will be (hopefully) for years to come. We can't wait to see how it grows and evolves into a fully fledged publishing platform. It's very exciting :)
