


Member location in Ghost Admin







































Ghost now registers the location your members sign up from, so you can see your members location on their member profile page in Ghost Admin.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2020/03/member-location-detail.png)

Understand your audience better, segment your members using [member labels](https://ghost.org/changelog/member-labels/) and build more relevant content for your readers!
