Discussion
----------

We're about to embark on a slight deviation from the sprint process we have been using. We're going to be building a couple of new experimental features, and will report progress and what we're planning next each week as it becomes clear.

The [full logs of the meeting](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1460478777001411?ref=ghost.org) can be found in our Slack logs.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm London time in the #dev channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.

Hope to see you at next week's meeting: [Tuesday 19th April, 5:30pm London time](https://everytimezone.com/?ref=ghost.org#2016-04-19,270,6bj).
