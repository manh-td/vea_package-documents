


Public Dev Meeting 17th Feb











































After a few quiet weeks, things are picking up again in Ghost land. We've been talking for a while about shipping our new navigation feature and in the meeting today we discussed the last few details. Those have now been listed on the [main issue](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/4535?ref=ghost.org#issuecomment-74725136), as a countdown to releasing the feature.

One of the main things left to decide is whether or not to include a default value (a link to home) or whether to leave the default blank and use a placeholder. Apart from this we're just wrapping up final details & testing.

During today's meeting we discussed and shelved a [proposal](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/4908?ref=ghost.org) to add a way to determine single vs multiple author blogs via the theme API.

There is now an [open & working PR](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/4853?ref=ghost.org) which switches the Ghost build system over to using [Ember-CLI](https://www.ember-cli.com/?ref=ghost.org), rather than the now deprecated Ember App Kit. This has a significant impact on our build steps like `grunt init` and we'd really appreciate getting people to test it out and let us know if it's working as expected on various setups.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm London time in the #ghost channel on freenode. In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.

Hope to see you at next week's meeting: [Tuesday 24th February, 5:30pm London time](https://everytimezone.com/?ref=ghost.org#2015-02-24,270,6bj).
