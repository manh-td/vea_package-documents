


Public Dev Meeting 22nd March











































This week is sprint change over week, so the meeting took place in hangout form. You can watch it back [on youtube](https://youtu.be/wslW0E_7MBw?ref=ghost.org) and see some corresponding chatter in the [#dev slack channel](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1458667938000487?ref=ghost.org).

Meeting Summary
---------------

In today's meeting we reviewed the progress of items from the [previous sprint](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6586?ref=ghost.org), and put together a new [sprint epic](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6627?ref=ghost.org). We've renewed efforts to get the sprint epics to be realistic and manageable given our other commitments.

We also discussed an [issue with timezones & permalinks](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6406?ref=ghost.org), which we need to plan a solution for before the existing PR can be merged.

Additionally there was some discussion around inline documentation in the code, the tools used to create this and what we might do with the output.

### Full details

If you'd like more details of what happened during the meeting, you can [on youtube](https://youtu.be/wslW0E_7MBw?ref=ghost.org).

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm UK time in the #dev channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.
