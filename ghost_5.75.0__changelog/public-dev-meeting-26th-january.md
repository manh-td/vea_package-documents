


Public Dev Meeting 26th January











































This week is sprint change over week, so the meeting took place in hangout form and you can watch it back [on youtube](https://youtu.be/i0o3O7-OCrk?ref=ghost.org) and see some corresponding chatter in the [#dev slack channel](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1453829710000269?ref=ghost.org).

Meeting Summary
---------------

In today's meeting we looked back at progress on the [last sprint](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6328?ref=ghost.org): the [post scheduling spec](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/wiki/Post-Scheduling-101?ref=ghost.org) is now on the wiki and the new editor demo has progressed significantly. Hashtags is still blocked on data amends, which needs finishing up.

We talked through the projects we intend to make progress on for the next 2 weeks:

* New Editor Demo
* Post Scheduling prep work
* Channels
* Welcome tour

You can see more details of the progress we're hoping to make in our [sprint epic](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6401?ref=ghost.org) on GitHub.

I also talked about some plans we have around adapters and introducing a CLI for Ghost, which will result in better tools for installs and upgrades. Over the next 2 weeks plans for that should be written up and ready for picking up and working on.

As always, there's a tonne to do so I'm gonna stop typing this and get cracking, hope to see you all next week!

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm UK time in the #dev channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.
