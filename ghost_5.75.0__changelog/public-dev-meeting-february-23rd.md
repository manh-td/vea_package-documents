


Public Dev Meeting February 23rd











































This week is sprint change over week, so the meeting took place in hangout form. You can watch it back [on youtube](https://youtu.be/Ef8esWTgFLc?ref=ghost.org) and see some corresponding chatter in the [#dev slack channel](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1455039081001272?ref=ghost.org).

Meeting Summary
---------------

In todays meeting we reviewed the progress of items from the [previous sprint](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6470?ref=ghost.org), and put together a new [sprint epic](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues/6560?ref=ghost.org).

With two releases and lots of internal work being done, not much progress was made on the actual sprint items themselves. Plenty of other PR's were merged though.

Many of the items from the last sprint have been pushed over to the current one. We are hoping to make further progress on 4 of the current projects and their dependencies during the next sprint (Editor, Hashtags, Post Scheduling, Tour).

A lot of community PRs have been merged, and more will be looked at. Meanwhile, there are still plenty of [other issues](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22fix+wanted%22&ref=ghost.org) which are available to be picked up and worked on.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm UK time in the #dev channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.
