Discussion
----------

* Current plan is to ship 0.7.2 early next week. The `{{get}}` helper will be moved under the labs flag as well and there should be a lookout for overall regression because of the many dependency updates. Tests of the software will continue to be added and optimized. The tags UI needs some testing and documentation on the `{{get}}` helper and public API will be created before the release ships.

The [full logs of the meeting](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1446572316000084?ref=ghost.org) can be found in our Slack logs.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm London time in the #dev channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.

Hope to see you at next week's meeting: [Tuesday 10th November, 5:30pm London time](https://everytimezone.com/?ref=ghost.org#2015-11-10,270,6bj).
