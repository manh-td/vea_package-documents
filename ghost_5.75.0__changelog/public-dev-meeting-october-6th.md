


Public Dev Meeting October 6th










































Quick Summary
-------------

Not much progress in the way of code, but some new announcements.

Progress Report
---------------

* Remove an API lookup for the active theme - [#5899](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/5899?ref=ghost.org)
* Code cleanup - [#5704](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/5704?ref=ghost.org), [#5909](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/5909?ref=ghost.org)

Priority Issues
---------------

* Everything in the [Post-Zelda](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+milestone%3APost-Zelda&ref=ghost.org) milestone are issues we would love to get done.

Discussion
----------

* The Ghost forum has been shutdown. The most popular and correct posts have been forwarded to the correct support doc, but the rest has been removed. If you need help with anything, let us know in the [Ghost slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org) channel and we can help you find it.
* Dev meetings will now start being in a Google hangout chat every 2 weeks to discuss and create a 'sprint' plan for the next 2 weeks. This hangout will be recorded and meeting notes will be posted beforehand on what will be covered. This will be a time to plan out the next 2 weeks and any discussions that arise. In the off weeks, there will be a written update in the #dev channel.

The [full logs of the meeting](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1444149044000082?ref=ghost.org) can be found in our Slack logs.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm London time in the #ghost channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.

Hope to see you at next week's meeting: [Tuesday 13th October, 5:30pm London time](https://everytimezone.com/?ref=ghost.org#2015-10-13,270,6bj).
