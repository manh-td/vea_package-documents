Quick Summary
-------------

Fixing a few bugs, getting issues raised, and deciding what needs to be in the next release as fixes.

Progress Report
---------------

* More improved test coverage - [#5814](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/5814?ref=ghost.org), [#5802](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/pull/5802?ref=ghost.org)

  + Anything else with the blocker label

Discussion
----------

We are hoping to have the blockers fixed and 0.7.1 released before the next meeting, but it may take a little more time.

If anyone is looking for anything to work on, the issues in the Post-Zelda milestone are things that would be great to get started on.

The [full logs of the meeting](https://ghost.slack.com/archives/dev/p1442334655000044?ref=ghost.org) can be found in our Slack logs.

What is this?
-------------

We hold a public development meeting pretty much every Tuesday at 5:30pm London time in the #ghost channel on Ghost's [slack](https://forum.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). In this meeting we discuss progress, important issues, and what is and isn't on [the Roadmap](https://trello.com/b/EceUgtCL/ghost-roadmap?ref=ghost.org). It's a chance for everyone to get involved and have their say about where Ghost goes next.

Hope to see you at next week's meeting: [Tuesday 22st September, 5:30pm London time](https://everytimezone.com/?ref=ghost.org#2015-09-22,270,6bj).
