


Quotes






































Now you can make quotes stand out with extra blockquote styles that can be applied directly in the editor.

In addition to the original quote style, which is minimal and left-aligned...

> The way to get started is to quit talking and begin doing – Walt Disney

Now there's a second style that you can use for larger, bold quotes.

> The only impossible journey is the one you never begin — Tony Robbins

Custom themes can also [implement](https://ghost.org/docs/themes/content/?ref=ghost.org#alternative-blockquote-style) alternative blockquote styles.
