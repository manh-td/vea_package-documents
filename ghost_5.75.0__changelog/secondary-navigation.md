


Secondary navigation







































Ghost Admin now supports secondary navigation, making it fast and simple to add or update links to your site's navigation menus. Head to the Design settings page in Ghost Admin to add your secondary navigation links:

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2019/12/secondary-navigation.png)

This means you can create a menu in your site's footer, or in the top right-hand corner. Find out how to add secondary navigation support in your theme in the [Handlebars Theme docs](https://ghost.org/docs/themes/helpers/navigation/?ref=ghost.org).
