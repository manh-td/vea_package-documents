


Segmented email newsletters






































It's now possible to deliver email newsletters to a specific group of members using custom segments, so you can provide an engaging and personalised experience to your audience.

![](https://ghost.org/changelog/content/images/2021/06/segmented-email.png)

The new options in the publishing menu allow you decide if your post is delivered by email to all members, free members, paid members or a specific group of people using labels.
