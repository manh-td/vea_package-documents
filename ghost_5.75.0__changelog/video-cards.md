


Video cards






































As a follow up from [audio cards](https://ghost.org/changelog/audio-cards), you can now upload videos to your content in Ghost. Once uploaded, your video content renders in a beautiful video player:

0:00/

Now you can share bonus video content for paid members, screenshares, demos, or clips from a full length feature.

Videos can be set to loop, have their own custom thumbnails, and display in three different widths using the editor toolbar.

0:00/🎬Video cards support files of up to **50mb** depending on your [Ghost(Pro) plan](https://ghost.org/pricing?ref=ghost.org), and accept `.mp4`, `WebM`, and `.ogg` filetypes.
