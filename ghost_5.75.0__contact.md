Contact
=======

Ghost is a fully distributed non-profit organisation registered in Singapore, with team members spread all over the world. We can be contacted by post at the following address:

> **Ghost Foundation**  
> 160 Robinson Road  
> #14-04 SBF Center

---

How to reach us
---------------

There are multiple ways to reach members of the Ghost team and the Ghost community depending on what you need some help with. Here's a list of the best ways to get in touch:

* For support using Ghost, the fastest way to get help is on our [forum](https://forum.ghost.org)
* **Ghost(Pro)** customers can always reach us on `support@ghost.org`
* To report security issues or vulnerabilities, follow our [responsible disclosure](/docs/security/#reporting-vulnerabilities) guide
* To report abuse of our services or members of our community, please follow our [code of conduct](/conduct/) and [abuse report](/abuse/) guidelines, which have more information about what to do.

Thanks for stopping by!
