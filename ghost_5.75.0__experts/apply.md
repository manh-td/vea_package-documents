What makes a Ghost Expert?
--------------------------

Ghost Experts are experienced third-party designers, developers and professionals.
Vetted for their specific experience with Ghost and the quality of work.

We vet all experts so that we can vouch the quality of the people we recommend, and charge a small annual fee based for being listed in the directory to cover the time taken to do the vetting.
