### Labs

* [Social web (beta)](https://ghost.org/help/social-web)


The Audience feedback feature gives your readers the opportunity to share feedback about your content when it matters most — directly *after* reading your newsletters.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2022/10/Email-Screenshot.png)

This feature can be setup within your newsletter settings. If you have multiple newsletters on your site, each can have a different setting for Audience feedback.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/08/edit-newsletter-settings.png)

From **Settings → Email Newsletter → Newsletters**, click the **Edit** button for the newsletter you wish to update. Under the **Design** tab, in the **Footer** section, **Ask your readers for feedback** allows you to toggle the Audience feedback on or off for that specific newsletter.

![The toggle switch for the newsletter feedback setting](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/10/audiencefeedback2.png)

When enabled, your audience can react to your content directly at the bottom of each newsletter you send. Feedback will appear in the [post analytics](https://ghost.org/help/post-analytics/) for each individual newsletter. You can also view individual member feedback, directly from a member profile in the [activity feed](https://ghost.org/help/dashboard/#recent-posts-and-member-activity).

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2022/10/Post-Analytics-More-Like-This.png)
