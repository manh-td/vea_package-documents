Once you have created your FirstPromoter account, go to the **Settings** menu and to the **Integrations** tab.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/04/FirstPromoter-settings.png)

Copy your **Account Id** and paste it into Ghost Admin on the **Integrations** page for FirstPromoter:

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/05/first-promoter-integration.png)

Hit **Save** and you're done! You can now start inviting referral partners to join your program so they can start recommending your publication to gain rewards!
