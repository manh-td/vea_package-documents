Ghost is a powerful, open-source publishing platform that enables you to create, publish, and monetize your content all in one place. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced user, this manual provides detailed instructions on everything from using Ghost's features to enhance your content, to setting up premium subscriptions and engaging with your audience.

Create your best work with a powerful visual editor that has familiar formatting options and the flexibility to add dynamic content like images, videos, buttons, embeds and more.

[Read more](https://ghost.org/help/using-the-editor/)![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/05/member.png)
#### Setting up memberships

Turn anonymous visitors into a thriving engaged community with memberships — build a loyal readership, offer exclusive content, and generate recurring revenue with subscriptions.

[Read more](https://ghost.org/help/setup-members/)![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/05/email.png)
#### Sending emails

Keep your audience in the loop with email newsletters. Learn how to segment your audience, create multiple newsletters, and create a branded email template.

[Read more](https://ghost.org/help/setup-email-newsletters/)![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2023/05/www.png)

Find out how to map any branded domain you own directly to your **Ghost(Pro)** publication, to make your site more memorable and easy to find.
