Enabling Tips & Donations in Ghost gives your readers an option to support your work with a one-off payment, without needing to sign up as a member, so long as you have an active [Stripe connection](https://ghost.org/help/stripe/) on your publication.

You can enable the Tips & Donations feature from the  **Settings → Growth → Tips & Donations** area within Ghost Admin.


0:00
/0:14









From there, you’ll be able to set a suggested amount for individuals to tip, and can set a custom currency.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/09/custom-currency-tips.png)

Once you've configured your suggested amount and currency, the payment link can be shared across your entire publication. For example, you can use your custom payment link within a button card, a custom header, as an email call to action, or you can even include your payment link within your site navigation.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/09/Ghost-tips-donations-nav.png)![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/09/Ghost-tips-donations-newsletter.png)![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/09/Ghost-tips-donations-page.png)

Tips & donations can be made by non-members, as well as existing members.

When a tip is received the publication owner, as well as any administrators role staff who have this [email notification](https://ghost.org/help/managing-your-team/#email-notifications) enabled in their staff user profile would receive an email.

![](https://ghost.org/help/content/images/2024/09/tip-notification-email.png)⚠️The tips & donations feature may not be available for every site, due to jurisdiction-specific prohibitions, as documented in [Stripe's Help Center](https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-for-accepting-tips-or-donations).
