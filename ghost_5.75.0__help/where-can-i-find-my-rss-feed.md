Ghost automatically generates RSS feeds for various areas of your publication.

For example, adding `/rss/` to most URLs on your Ghost publication produces an automatically generated `rss` feed, for example:

* **Main post index** - <https://demo.ghost.io/rss/>
* **Author archive** - <https://demo.ghost.io/author/lewis/rss/>
* **Tag archive** - <https://demo.ghost.io/tag/fiction/rss/>

If your site is in [Private Site Mode](https://ghost.org/help/site-setup/#make-site-private), the RSS feed is not publicly available, however, you can retrieve a private RSS feed URL from within Ghost Admin in the **Settings →  General →  Make this site private** area.
