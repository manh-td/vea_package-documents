Integrate Ghost with Buttondown to ensure your readers can subscribe to your newsletter content

[Buttondown.email](https://buttondown.email/) is a email newsletter solution that allows you to focus on writing a great newsletter in an editor of your choice by allowing you to paste some Markdown or HTML into the tool and send it to your subscribers.

If you’re using Buttondown to power your email newsletter, you’ll probably want to embed a subscription widget onto your Ghost publication. Here’s how:

Copy the URL of your newsletter
-------------------------------

Grab the URL of the newsletter you’d like to embed a subscription widget for onto your post or page

![](/images/integrations/buttondown-example_hu17eec9320bb81087481399890e6ab05b_212552_2000x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Paste it into the Ghost editor
------------------------------

When you paste it into the Ghost editor it’ll be automatically transformed into a rich embed of the subscription widget:

![](/images/integrations/card-render_hu2c78047432353a9629cd84520e241107_6034_1694x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Publish your post
-----------------

That’s all there is to it! Ghost interacts with Buttondown via their [OEmbed](https://oembed.com/) API in order to retrieve all the correct settings automatically and serve your widget in the best way possible.
