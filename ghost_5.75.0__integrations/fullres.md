[Analytics](/integrations/?tag=Analytics)

Powerful real-time insights and detailed reports on your publications’s performance.

With [fullres](https://fullres.com), you get detailed analytics that help you understand your audience better, track engagement, and optimize your content strategy. It’s super lightweight and GDPR friendly.

Sign up for fullres & copy your tracking code
---------------------------------------------

If you haven’t already, [create an account on fullres](https://app.fullres.com/accounts/new). Once registered, you’ll be able to access your unique tracking code.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a068b234-eba3-4eb1-a10f-a5c331d2220b)

Add tracking using Ghost code injection
---------------------------------------

In Ghost Admin, go to **Settings -> Code Injection**. Paste the fullres tracking code into the **Footer** section and hit save.

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/pCHX0R2jaQLAj5I6mWlEJOU00s0v32KOFNu_7qeFt9S4exU-oTpexh-W8hBcJDkgP0Z6XbWvg41_Ay6W4nmXvXxbscz-Eur8B6548Gj6oK4MDDvk-3ZS14ZffsznaT5RTR5FmWlt)

Your site is now integrated with fullres. Sign in to your fullres dashboard to start monitoring your site’s performance.

Advanced Customization and Enrichment
-------------------------------------

Beyond basic web analytics, fullres can optionally also provide detailed, granular data about your members, authors, and site categories.

[Head over to the fullres blog for a detailed set of tutorials](https://fullres-demo.ghost.io/)
