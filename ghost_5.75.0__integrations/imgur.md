Embed content from Imgur in Ghost to share the most awesome images on the internet with your readers

Imgur has one of the largest libraries of the internet’s most popular photos and gifs, and all of them can be freely embedded on any Ghost site. There are only a couple of steps to get Imgur photos working within your posts.

To get started, head over to [Imgur](https://imgur.com) and find the photo you’d like to use.

Select a photo and click embed
------------------------------

Open the share menu from the three dots and select `Embed Post`

![](/images/integrations/imgur-embed_hu2e89e72d9ca28f1999ac679a8f6262aa_199607_1202x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Copy the Imgur embed code
-------------------------

From within the embed window, click on the `Copy` button to copy the code.

![](/images/integrations/imgur-embed-code_hu19effde9b62b73dc4b07d2349e725c71_51272_1212x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Within the Ghost editor, add a new HTML card
--------------------------------------------

You can paste the form code within an HTML card on any page to embed your form

![](/images/integrations/Ghost-cards_huedc570934e065d9fb5514b16c7852436_24138_1872x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Enjoy your new Imgur integration
--------------------------------

That’s it — your embed will now appear within your post. To move the embed around your post, simply drag and drop the HTML card. When you hit publish, your embed will appear in your content. Have fun!
