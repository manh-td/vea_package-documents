It’s possible to embed podcast content from Listen Notes directly on your Ghost site via our OEmbed integration.

If you’re hosting any kind of audio content, it’s important to use a reliable service with high-quality performance. If you want to feature podcast content on a Ghost site, it’s possible to use a specialised provider for audio content, and embed their audio player to your site.

Ghost is fully compatible with [Listen Notes](https://www.listennotes.com/), a popular podcast search engine where you can search for podcasts or curate your own playlists.

Copy the URL of a podcast page
------------------------------

Copy and paste the url of a podcast page ([Example](https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/secret-leaders-dan-murray-rich-martell-DCi0fQuTz2c/)), an episode page ([Example](https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/secret-leaders/clara-brenner-the-vc-rMLQxR4CE2T/)), a clip page ([Example](https://www.listennotes.com/clips/how-we-podcast-JrIELDQ2CM8/)), or a Listen Later playlist page ([Example](https://www.listennotes.com/listen/voices-of-women-in-venture-QYX4qEdwUml/?display=episode)) to the Ghost editor.

![](/images/integrations/ghost-insert-oembed-link_hu2c78047432353a9629cd84520e241107_6034_1694x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

It will automatically unfurl the Listen Notes player using our OEmbed integration.

Use embed codes
---------------

For further customisation of your embedded content, you can also use the embed code from Listen Notes.

![](/images/integrations/listen-notes-get-embed-codes-in-ghost_hu755ba4a318255ea84bb9158927bf2453_29290_1754x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Once you’ve copied the code, paste it into an HTML card in the Ghost editor. From here you can adjust or add styling to suit your needs:

![](/images/integrations/listen-notes-embed-codes-in-ghost_hucd9619aae27db926d04b86b261fc6baa_63016_1716x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Automate with Zapier
--------------------

It’s also possible to automatically create a post in Ghost when a new episode is added to your Listen Later playlists, using [Zapier](https://ghost.org/integrations/zapier). To do this, use the **RSS by Zapier** action step, using the [RSS feed for your Listen Later playlist](https://help.listennotes.com/en/articles/3409251-how-to-subscribe-to-my-listen-later-playlists):

![](/images/integrations/listen-notes-zap-rss-feed_hu9f93ff92d89b5c05af26ddd17dbc8e9e_29527_1832x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)
