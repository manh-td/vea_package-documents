[Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) is an extendable launcher for MacOS. It’s a successor to Alfred and a replacement for Spotlight. In a nutshell, it’s a super efficient way to open files and apps, manage windows, jump into your next meeting, convert and transform values, and so much more.

The official Ghost extension for Raycast provides fuzzy, typeahead search across all of our documentation.

![Ghost Raycast integration](/images/integrations/ghost-docs-1_hu1e1c29cacb4d457aabcea8c7b253ba7d_2414355_2000x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

That means you have instant access to our [help center](https://ghost.org/help/), [developer docs](https://ghost.org/docs/), [tutorials](https://ghost.org/tutorials/), [changelog](https://ghost.org/changelog/), and [publisher resources](https://ghost.org/resources/). You can also narrow your search to only return results from a particular source.

Install the extension
---------------------

Head over to the [Raycast store to install the extension](https://www.raycast.com/ryan_feigenbaum/ghost-docs).
