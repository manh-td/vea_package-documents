All content from Senja works with Ghost automatically via our oEmbed integration. All you have to do is paste a URL!

[Senja](https://senja.io/?ref=ghost) is a free tool to automate your testimonial and case study collection, manage them all in one place and share them everywhere. Free forever.

Copy the URL of your Senja component
------------------------------------

Grab the URL of the [Senja](https://senja.io/?ref=ghost) component you’d like to embed into your post or page

![](/images/integrations/senja1_hu59f75a80b53b6c5aff139d5d3b1e7d21_1502785_1800x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Paste it into the Ghost editor
------------------------------

When you paste it into the Ghost editor it’ll be automatically transformed into a rich embed of the component you selected

![](/images/integrations/card-render_hu2c78047432353a9629cd84520e241107_6034_1694x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Publish your post
-----------------

That’s all there is to it! Ghost pulls in your Senja component via their [oEmbed](https://oembed.com/) API in order to retrieve all the correct settings automatically and display it in the best way possible.
