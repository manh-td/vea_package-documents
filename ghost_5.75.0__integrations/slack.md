Notify Slack when a new post is published so everyone knows when new content goes live and your team stays in sync

Create a new incoming webhook in Slack
--------------------------------------

First, you’ll need to set up a new [incoming webhook](https://my.slack.com/apps/new/A0F7XDUAZ-incoming-webhooks) in your team’s Slack configuration. An incoming webhook is a method for Slack to receive incoming messages to be posted to your Slack team from external services.

![](/images/integrations/slack-incoming-webhook_hu06a862a1e5bdac401d60f0ed21c64665_60717_1974x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Customise webhook & copy URL
----------------------------

Once the webhook is created, you can optionally customise the channel, name, and icon which the webhook uses whenever it posts a new message. Either way, however, you’ll need to copy the `Webhook URL` at the very top.

![](/images/integrations/slack-customise-webhook_hu26a8aa09a4c87851b45be5f405576cdc_109984_2000x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Add your Slack webhook to Ghost
-------------------------------

Inside Ghost Admin, head over to the **Integrations** page and click on the Slack integration settings. Here, you can paste your webhook URL, save the settings, and you’re all done! You can also hit the **Send test notification** button to make sure everything is working correctly.

![Ghost Admin Slack integration](/images/integrations/slack-ghost-integration_huc84a903cb05ee1e91bbbdd5ccfe42771_55705_1098x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

From here on, your team will receive Slack notifications each time a new post is published on your site 🎉

More automation with Zapier
---------------------------

You can also go further and connect other popular Ghost integrations to your Slack team using Zapier - so you get an overview of new comments, subscribers or contact form submissions right in your workspace.

It’s also possible to create an automation to invite new [Members](/help/members-introduction/) in Ghost to a private Slack group - which means you can offer a private community as a perk to your most loyal subscribers.
