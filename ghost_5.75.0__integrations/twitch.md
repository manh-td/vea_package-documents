All content from Twitch works with Ghost automatically via our OEmbed integration. All you have to do is paste a URL!

If you’re already streaming to [Twitch](https://twitch.tv), then you’ll probably want a way to embed past videos onto your site to build up a shareable archive. Here’s a quick guide for making Twitch and Ghost work together:

Copy the URL of the video
-------------------------

Grab the URL of the video you’d like to embed into your post or page

![](/images/integrations/twitch-url_hu42f6244ca407dc1a35253f231512cb37_130850_1544x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Paste it into the Ghost editor
------------------------------

When you paste it into the Ghost editor it’ll be automatically transformed into a rich embed of the video you selected

![](/images/integrations/card-render_hu2c78047432353a9629cd84520e241107_6034_1694x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)

Publish your post
-----------------

That’s all there is to it! Ghost interacts with Twitch via their [OEmbed](https://oembed.com/) API in order to retrieve all the correct settings automatically and serve your video in the best way possible.
