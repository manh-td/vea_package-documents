It's time to write a new(s) story.
----------------------------------

Amidst all the struggles of modern media and its place in an ever-changing society, we see opportunity.

Advertising models may never return to their heyday, but reader-driven, subscription-funded models are re-aligning incentives and bringing communities back together.

Quality, rather than quantity, is what works in 2024.

#### How The Atlantic went from broke to profitable in 3 years

We believe there’s a huge untapped audience for the highest quality journalism that is still willing to pay for it.

[The Wall Street Journal →](https://www.wsj.com/business/media/how-the-atlantic-went-from-broke-to-profitable-in-three-years-03cc3b18)![Feature images](/images/vertical/news-story2_hud3eab507bc63a51ba44887479dfc34c4_80521_840x0_resize_q100_h2_box_3.webp)
#### Lessons from a $4.5M ad-free news platform in Holland

We’ve grown to an online journalism platform with more than 60,000 paying members. Readers pay us, and we invest that money into journalism. That is our business model.

[De Correspondent →](https://medium.com/de-correspondent/how-we-turned-a-world-record-in-journalism-crowd-funding-into-an-actual-publication-2a06e298afe1)![Feature images](/images/vertical/news-story3_hua58c9c07ab028879a69ee2ca7d657cf2_104611_840x0_resize_q100_h2_box.webp)
#### Why the future of media is subscription-based

In the ever-evolving landscape of media, a seismic shift is underway, and subscription-based models are emerging as the linchpin of the industry's future.

[Forbes →](https://www.forbes.com/sites/falonfatemi/2023/12/13/why-the-future-of-media-is-subscription-based/)

We work with local news publishers and independent journalists all over the world to help them build a sustainable business to fund their reporting.

The good news? It's working.

Today, Ghost is is one of the most widely adopted platforms in the world for independent news. There are far more news sites running on Ghost than on Newspack, at a fraction of the cost.
