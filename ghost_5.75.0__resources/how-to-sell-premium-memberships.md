




How to sell premium memberships








































For creators and aspiring entrepreneurs looking to generate a sustainable recurring revenue stream from their creative work, Ghost has built-in payments that allow you to create a subscription commerce business.

You only need to create a [Stripe](https://ghost.org/help/stripe/?ref=ghost.org) account and connect this to Ghost. From there, you can create monthly and yearly premium billing cadences, add multiple tiers, and offer all this to your visitors via Portal.

The best part: Ghost takes **0% payment fees**, so everything you make is yours to keep!

Using subscriptions, you can build an independent media business like [The Browser](https://thebrowser.com/?ref=ghost.org), [The Lever](https://www.levernews.com/?ref=ghost.org), or [Creator Science](https://creatorscience.com/?ref=ghost.org).
