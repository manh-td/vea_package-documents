****💸 Business models****
-------------------------

### [How publishers are playing with time in their paywall strategies](https://www.twipemobile.com/how-publishers-playing-time-paywall-strategies/?ref=ghost.org)

"Recent research from INMA shows that globally only [39% of national media](https://www.inma.org/blogs/reader-revenue/post.cfm/research-47-of-paywalls-are-freemium-models?ref=ghost.org) outlets charge for their digital content. Of these, 47% of the paywalls use the freemium model, in which most content is available to everyone but select premium content is available only for paying subscribers."

### [Digital news and media subscriptions grow 110% in 3 months: What’s next for publishers](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/digital-news-and-media-subscriptions-grow-110-in-3-months-whats-next-for-publishers/?ref=ghost.org)

"Businesses relying on subscription revenues continue to show resilience through the pandemic. 50% are growing as fast as they were before the pandemic, and 18% have shown accelerated growth (greater than 25%), according to Zuora’s latest edition of its [Subscription Impact Report](https://www.zuora.com/resource/subscription-impact-report/?ref=ghost.org)."

### [Big publishers are using puzzles as a way to increase retention](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/compelling-way-to-invite-readers-to-return-often-to-publishers-websites-why-puzzles-are-increasingly-featuring-in-reader-retention-strategies/?ref=ghost.org)

It seems the same strategies newspapers have used for decades work on the internet too! The NYT adding 119k subscribers who signed up for its crosswords, cooking and audio products.
