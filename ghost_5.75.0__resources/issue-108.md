




#108 — Passion communities are flourishing







































This week we've got an interesting success story, why newsletters are surging, some free resources for publishers from Google, a webinar about being an independent journalist and more. We hope you enjoy this issue!

****💯 Top picks****
-------------------

### [Nathan Tankus's newsletter subscribers don’t care about diplomas](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-07-02/nathan-tankus-s-newsletter-subscribers-don-t-care-about-diplomas?ref=ghost.org)

With 450 subscribers generating $45,000 in annual revenue, Nathan Tankus has carved a niche as one of the most popular newsletter publishers writing about economics and finance, with plenty of economists and journalists following and subscribing to Nathan's work, [Notes on the Crises](https://nathantankus.substack.com/?ref=ghost.org).

****💸 Business models****
-------------------------

### [Passion communities are flourishing](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/why-passion-communities-are-flourishing-during-lockdown-and-what-this-means-for-social-networks/?ref=ghost.org)

"The surge in popularity of passion communities largely boils down to two factors: time and opportunity."

### [50 lessons from writing 50 newsletters](https://nesslabs.com/50-lessons-50-newsletters?ref=ghost.org)

Anne-Laure Le Cunff shares plenty of insights about what it's like to write an email newsletter after publishing the 50th issue of [Maker Mind](https://nesslabs.com/newsletter?ref=ghost.org).

### [Steady subscription strategies for publishers: “This is a full-blown transformation”](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/this-is-a-full-blown-transformation-steady-subscription-strategies-for-publishers/?ref=ghost.org)

Content consumption is growing and people are more willing to pay for content they find worthwhile. This article investigates how this is a full-blown transformation, rather than a passing trend. *"The TL;DR is that this is happening, and it seems like it’s here to stay."*

### [Reuters plans to put many articles behind a paywall](https://talkingbiznews.com/they-talk-biz-news/reuters-plans-to-put-many-articles-behind-a-paywall/?ref=ghost.org)

"Reuters would become one of the last free news services to adopt a subscription business model, a sign of how much the news industry has shifted away from relying on advertising."

### [‘If your enemy has all the power, you may as well sleep with them’: Why subscription publishers can’t quit Facebook ads](https://digiday.com/media/if-your-enemy-has-all-the-power-you-may-as-well-sleep-with-them-why-subscription-publishers-cant-quit-facebook-ads/?ref=ghost.org)

"It’s clear to see why publishers advertise on Facebook, even if they say they don’t like it very much."

### [Why the email newsletter is 2020’s MVP format](https://www.rjionline.org/stories/why-the-email-newsletter-is-2020s-mvp-format?ref=ghost.org)

"Newsletters are surging, and it’s not just a COVID thing"

### [Creating reader habits with digital editions](https://www.twipemobile.com/how-digital-editions-helping-create-reader-habits/?ref=ghost.org)
