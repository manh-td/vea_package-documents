### [The aftermath of the biggest rebellion in online media](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/07/28/business/media/deadspin-staffers-start-defector.html?ref=ghost.org)

All of the journalists working at the sports-centric website Deadspin resigned as a protest after clashing with bosses. Now, 18 of the 20 who quit have started their own media company, that they will own and operate themselves. It's called Defactor Media and has [attracted more than 10,000 subscribers](https://twitter.com/defectormedia/status/1288496561077587968?ref=ghost.org) on day one.
