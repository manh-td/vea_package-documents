### [[Podcast] Pricing strategies for uncertain times](https://hbr.org/podcast/2020/07/pricing-strategies-for-uncertain-times?ref=ghost.org)

### [111% more article clicks: Facebook’s new feature can help publishers build engagement](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/111-more-article-clicks-how-facebooks-new-feature-can-help-publishers-build-engagement/?ref=ghost.org)

"Facebook is testing a [new feature](https://www.facebook.com/journalismproject/publisher-account-linking) that allows paying news subscribers to link their Facebook accounts to their subscriptions. Once they have done that, such users will not be required to log in to their news subscription account in order to view articles from those publishers."
