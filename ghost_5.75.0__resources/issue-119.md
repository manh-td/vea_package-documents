




#119 — The high privacy cost of free websites & notes on membership







































This week: More ideas about membership, more journalists carving their own path and more tips about building community and growing your newsletter. Enjoy this weeks reading/listening and feel free to forward to a friend!

****💯 Top picks****
-------------------

### [Notes on membership](https://pressthink.org/2020/09/notes-on-membership/?ref=ghost.org)

Last week the Membership Puzzle Project shared a new [guide](https://membershipguide.org/?ref=ghost.org) on memberships. This short collection of notes from the director of that project, Jay Rosen, is a great insight into the differences between subsidy systems (like advertising and government funded journalism) and direct payments via subscriptions.

****💸 Business models****
-------------------------

### [Casey Newton on leaving The Verge and the future of tech journalism](https://onezero.medium.com/casey-newton-on-leaving-the-verge-for-substack-and-the-future-of-tech-journalism-974a646375fa?ref=ghost.org)

Sarah Jeong talks to Newton about the details of his deal, subscription journalism, and what makes email such a good media format

### [The art of retention through community building](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/publishers-have-attracted-more-readers-during-the-pandemic-and-seen-subscriptions-boom-the-art-of-retention-through-community-building/?ref=ghost.org)

*"*It’s time for publishers to take their brands off the page and screen, and integrate them into people’s lives to keep the subscribers they’ve attracted during the pandemic and build long-term loyalty, says Hayley James, Vice President at Sense New York.*"*

### [How I ran an NPR-style pledge drive to grow my newsletter](https://simonowens.substack.com/p/how-i-ran-an-npr-style-pledge-drive?ref=ghost.org)

"The specific strategies can vary, but in most cases the publisher, for some period of time ranging anywhere from a week to a month, will cut its subscription prices while simultaneously bombarding the audience with a large volume of messages encouraging them to subscribe."

### [In reader revenue, it’s all about who you know](http://editorandpublisher.com/stories/in-reader-revenue-model-its-all-about-who-you-know,172665?ref=ghost.org)

"For news organizations betting the future on consumer revenue, there might not be a more important key performance indicator than *known readers*."
