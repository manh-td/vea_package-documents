




#170 — How to unbundle your way to an audience








































*Want to get featured below? [Submit](https://ghost.org/resources/submit) an article.*

Unbundling means to take a slice of your competitor's pie, one small aspect, and do it better than them. In this week's issue, dive into the creators and companies doing exactly that, and winning big as a result.

### [On publishing 100 articles in 100 days and crossing $100K ARR: Anne-Laure Le Cunff's story](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/on-publishing-100-articles-in-100-days-and-crossing-100k-arr-anne-laure-le-cunffs-story-f99d8130c9?ref=ghost.org)

> Consistency trumps strategy.

Anne-Laure Le Cunff was recently profiled on Indie Hackers where they shared their story of growing a newsletter and community to six figures in under two years. The piece is filled with practical advice, such as how the author balanced their side project with full-time work, how they chose what to work on, and what advice they have for those just starting out.

**Related:** [How to make it as an artist: Stop following the rules](https://jessicaabel.com/how-to-make-it-as-an-artist/?ref=ghost.org) — Autonomous Creative

### [A labor movement for the platform economy](https://hbr.org/2021/09/a-labor-movement-for-the-platform-economy?ref=ghost.org)

Li Jin explains how the future of independent and creative work will rely on individuals taking more control of the platforms they utilize. The article cites the recent protests of DoorDash and Twitch as examples of how users can influence the algorithms they work with. Still, [owning your platform](https://ghost.org/alternatives/?ref=ghost.org) is the best way to build a sustainable income online.

**Related:** [It's time for new marketing metaphors](https://contentfolks.substack.com/p/aggressive-marketing-metaphors?ref=ghost.org) — Contentfolks

### [Single-topic news organizations are “a growing niche” in nonprofit news](https://www.niemanlab.org/2021/09/single-topic-news-organizations-are-a-growing-niche-in-nonprofit-news/?ref=ghost.org)

> The number of single-topic nonprofit news organizations has quadrupled since 2008.

It used to be that businesses won because of their size and breadth. Newspapers, cable networks, and even technology companies won by having their hand in as many segments of the market as possible. Now, the opposite is true. Ben Thompson calls it [The Great Unbundling](https://youtu.be/UUsoGDqjVsQ?ref=ghost.org). Expect this trend to grow as even more populations come online for the first time.

****📝 Modern** publishing**
---------------------------

### [How Axios is tackling local news: newsletters from small teams, in more markets](https://digiday.com/media/how-axios-is-tackling-local-news-newsletters-from-small-teams-in-more-markets/?ref=ghost.org)

Instead of building large, centralized teams, Axios aims to build a legion of small, localized newsrooms. This approach will allow them to keep costs down while expanding their reach into new markets quickly. This is another perfect example of the idea that as the internet gets bigger, one should think smaller.

**Related:** [How Social Spider is making local news commercially viable](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/the-demand-is-definitely-there-how-social-spider-is-making-local-news-commercially-viable/?ref=ghost.org) — WNiP

### [Time partners with Charter to expand into future of work coverage](https://www.axios.com/time-charter-partnership-future-of-work-802888c3-4989-4413-a2f2-525475c2c1d4.html?ref=ghost.org)

Traditional publishing is willing to experiment more than ever before. Time announced they'd be partnering with Charter to "distribute its content across Time's owned and operated channels." It's worth noting that this is not an acquisition, as they wrote there is "no financial exchange in this partnership." However, the collaboration certainly lays the groundwork for future monetizable endeavors.

**Related:** [Leah Finnegan is rebuilding Gawker with her editorial vision front and center](https://digiday.com/media/its-not-going-to-be-nice-leah-finnegan-is-rebuilding-gawker-with-her-editorial-vision-front-and-center/?ref=ghost.org) — Digiday

It's a useful guide for those that want to better leverage social media and digital communities.

### [How publishers can use audio to spark greater audience engagement](https://www.journalism.co.uk/news/how-publishers-can-use-audio-to-promote-greater-audience-engagement/s2/a862965/?ref=ghost.org)

### [Google Search to get 1,000 times more powerful](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/mums-the-word-google-search-to-get-1000-times-more-powerful/?ref=ghost.org)

> With this new capability, a user can look for something that might be difficult to describe accurately with words alone.

Google recently announced "a new technology called Multitask Unified Model, or MUM for short" that will help them move into the future of search. Essentially, AI will help users search for things using images, words, and combinations of the two. The goal is to help people find what they're looking for in "fewer searches." It's a fascinating improvement that will be sure to impact how content is made in the years to come.

### [How to drive business results with analytics](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/9-criteria-for-publishers-to-drive-business-results-with-analytics-in-2022/?ref=ghost.org)

What's New in Publishing identifies nine action steps publishers can take to capture better analytics in a cookie-less future. They include focusing on fewer data points, investing in strategic tag management, and working with external analytics experts.
