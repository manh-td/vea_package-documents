### [This publisher tested a third party personalisation vendor with disappointing results](https://digiday.com/media/aller-media-personalized-paywall/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [Thread: Restoring trust should be the number one priority in journalism and membership can help](https://twitter.com/robwijnberg/status/1052194833777840128?utm_campaign=1d802c524b-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-1d802c524b-396331633)

The founder shared some more interesting thoughts about the future of subscriptions in this thread - we love a good Twitter thread!

### [How a subscription video service doubles its subscribers by owning a genre](https://digiday.com/media/kind-genre-shudders-content-strategy-keeps-subscribers-coming-back/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Shudder, a subscription video service for horror movies fans, has seen compounding subscription growth by taking ownership of their niche and combining horror classics with original content.
