💯 Top picks
-----------

### [Improving reader retention: How Nordic publisher Schibsted Media reduces churn](https://digiday.com/media/readers-drive-40-percent-schibsted-revenue/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Schibsted Media first put up their paywall 15 years ago, and are now generating 40 percent of revenue from memberships. To keep these paying members around, they’ve been building topic-specific customer journey models and learning what content drives retention.

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [The Athletic are pulling investors away from ad companies with a $40 million series C funding round](https://www.axios.com/the-athletic-40-million-series-c-round-9585ff32-c007-4212-ae31-913df625aa9a.html?utm_campaign=223696e319-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-223696e319-396331633)

“Our revenue comes in recurring payments. It’s a lot less explosive than ad companies — it’s harder to build — but the payoff is huge in revenue foundation.” *– The Athletic co-founder Alex Mather*

### [Using product testing to increase subscriptions and reader consumption at The Wall Street Journal](https://digiday.com/media/3-million-subscribers-wall-street-journal-focuses-product-testing/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

A six-person team is performing dozens of tests to get The Wall Street Journal’s and Barron’s audiences to read more and subscribe more!

### [This startup is going to offer ad-free news from a collection of large news outlets for $5 a month!](http://www.niemanlab.org/2018/10/hunting-for-reader-revenue-scroll-sets-up-shop-for-2019-with-more-publishers-and-10-million-raised/?utm_campaign=197a00de83-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-197a00de83-396331633)

Tony Haile, Chartbeat’s founding CEO, has raised millions of dollars and made partnerships with several publishers for his startup [Scroll](https://scroll.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter), which offers $5 per-month subscriptions for an ad-free experience from a variety of news outlets. It’s set to launch in the first quarter of 2019!

### [The paywall that saved The New York Times from decline has room to grow yet!](http://www.niemanlab.org/2018/11/the-new-york-times-is-on-pace-to-earn-more-than-600-million-in-digital-this-year-halfway-to-its-ambitious-goal/?utm_campaign=272b8198f2-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-272b8198f2-396331633)

In years gone by, The New York Times generated 80 percent of the revenue in the *entire American newspaper industry -* which has dropped significantly in recent years to 45 percent. However, the news outlet is still on pace to earn more than $600 million in digital with subscriptions leading the charge!
