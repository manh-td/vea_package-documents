### [Editions vs Newsflow: How do readers prefer to consume their news?](https://medium.com/global-editors-network/daily-editions-vs-continuous-newsflow-how-do-users-prefer-news-today-67a998673562?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Global Editors Network and the publishing analytics company [Twipe](https://www.twipemobile.com/about-us/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)surveyed 4,000 news consumers across Europe and the United States to find out how people like to read news. Much like the political landscape, they discovered an even split amongst readers who prefer to consume a “*newsflow*” format compared to an edition format.
