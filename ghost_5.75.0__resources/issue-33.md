### [THREAD: Publications aren't cutting jobs because they're losing audience. They're cutting jobs because of tech!](https://twitter.com/bedwardstiek/status/1084375052865294336?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [The FT is building brand loyalty among young readers by giving schools free access](https://digiday.com/media/financial-times-building-brand-loyalty-among-young-readers/?utm_campaign=f72fc62f1f-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-f72fc62f1f-396331633)

The Financial Times runs a program that lifts the paywall for participating schools to give young readers access to their content. The publisher is cultivating relationships with teachers via an email newsletter to promote engagement with the program.

--------------

### [No, tech companies shouldn’t fund journalism](https://www.cjr.org/opinion/no-facebook-shouldnt-fund-journalism.php?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Facebook recently announced that it would spend $300 million in the next three years funding journalism partnerships and programs. This opinion piece on Columbia Journalism Review discusses how this could be bad for journalism as a whole.

### [Netflix is finally sharing (some of) its audience numbers. Several of them are huge.](https://www.recode.net/2019/1/17/18187234/netflix-views-numbers-first-time-bird-box-bodyguard-you-sex-education?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)
