💯 Top picks

### [Subscriptions, subsidies, and pressure on platforms: media trends in 2019](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/risj-review/subscriptions-subsidies-and-pressure-platforms-media-trends-2019?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The news industry is refocusing attention away from Facebook and over 52% of those who participated in this Reuters Institute study expect subscriptions and memberships to be the focus this year. There are also some worries that these funding models will have some limitations, and there will need to be more subsidies for quality news. Full report [here](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/our-research/journalism-media-and-technology-trends-and-predictions-2019?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)!

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Building subscriptions and memberships for quality journalism: How Tribune Publishing, The Guardian, and Slate did it](https://www.lenfestinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Lenfest-DCN-Reader-Revenue-Case-Studies.pdf?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The Lenfest Institute have published a 16-page study backed with data from Slate, The Guardian and Tribune Publishing about how they’ve each tackled reader revenue with a strong focus on valuing good journalism.

### [Magazine publishers race to capitalise on the paywall trend](https://www.foliomag.com/subscription-marketing-paywall/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Amid changing consumer perceptions toward paid content, publishers embrace both new and tested methods for marketing subscriptions and reducing churn.”

### [Texas Monthly launch a metered paywall](https://www.texasmonthly.com/the-stand-up-desk/a-letter-to-our-readers/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Here’s an example of a publication taking ownership of their business model and simply asking their readers to pay for quality content. Dan Goodgame, Editor in Chief at Texas Monthly, wrote this letter to their readers to *“do what our print subscribers have done for 46 years”*.

### [Medium is spinning up 4 new independent publications](https://digiday.com/media/medium-prepping-four-subscription-publications-come/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

If this sounds familiar, it’s because it’s the same thing they did - in some form - with ****The Awl****, ****The Hairpin****, ****The Ringer****, ****Pacific Standard****, ****Backchannel****, ****Matter****, ****Electric Lit****, ****Femsplain****, ****The Billfold****, ****The Development Set****, and more — all of which were subsequently [spun-out, laid-off, or shut down](http://www.niemanlab.org/2018/01/hurry-up-before-its-gone-new-york-city-the-awl-and-the-hairpin-are-shutting-down/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) with little-to-no warning. So many people are becoming [increasingly skeptical](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/daveyalba/ev-williams-wants-to-save-media-again-but-some-writers-and?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) about the approach.

### [Why tech platforms need to be built on journalistic values](https://niemanreports.org/articles/why-tech-platforms-need-to-be-built-on-journalistic-values/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“It’s the reason your feed is overrun with click-bait, why serious or controversial stories lack context, why stories don’t receive proper attribution, and stories with false information gain momentum. This is what we’re facing right now.”

### [First signs of a subscription model for podcasts](https://www.twipemobile.com/pivot-paid-podcasts-following-subscription-strategy/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [Verification tools: Placing trust in humans, robots or both of them?](https://medium.com/global-editors-network/verification-tools-placing-trust-in-humans-robots-or-both-of-them-b928ee9a36f2?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The Global Editors Network’s programme are building a free Media Literacy Toolkit for Newsrooms around Europe to help journalists take ownership of their work and fight misinformation, with inspiration from other tools such as [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) and [Factmata](https://factmata.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter).
