💸 Business models
-----------------

### [What can media learn from other member-driven movements?](https://membershippuzzle.org/articles-overview/wha-media-can-learn?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*“Journalism’s traditional financial models are dying. Could churches, environmental movements, and open source communities hold clues to its survival?”*

Membership Puzzle Project teamed up with the Knight Centre to research this topic and shared their findings in this [report](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/58cfa917d2b85767b5778f0a/t/5cb05c5da4222f112b9b852b/1555061880123/mpp_report_US_01.pdf?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter). Their advice? Listen to your members, offer flexible means of participation and grow at a human scale. [****See also****: A [webinar](https://vimeo.com/271778558?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) on the same topic].

### [Are newspapers keeping their subscribers hostage? [Podcast]](https://rework.fm/subscription-hostages/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The Rework podcast from Basecamp takes a look at the consumer trap of *subscription hostages* and why so many services & products don’t provide an easy way for people to cancel, or have any confidence in what they’re selling.

### [Whereby.Us have converted one percent its newsletter subscribers to $10/mo members](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/04/experience-experiments-what-whereby-uss-membership-model-looks-like-after-adding-a-spirited-media-site/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“For us, it’s about being able to provide an extra layer of community to our members, to the people who are really engaged with us, support the work that we do, and want to get engaged in the city,” CEO [Chris Sopher](https://twitter.com/cksopher?lang=en&utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) said.

👩‍💻 Technology
--------------

### [Is YouTube is turning its back on independent creators?](https://www.theverge.com/2019/4/5/18287318/youtube-logan-paul-pewdiepie-demonetization-adpocalypse-premium-influencers-creators?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Many highly successful creators have emerged on YouTube in recent years, and the platform has supported this and helped them to go viral. But as the video giant heads towards being more like television, it could end up leaving its humble independent creators behind!

### [We’ll finally get to see what Luminary, the paywalled podcast(ish?) app, has been cooking](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/04/well-finally-get-to-see-what-luminary-the-paywalled-podcastish-app-has-been-cooking/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The latest issue of [Hot Pod](https://hotpodnews.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) reports that Luminary will launch later this month with an $8 a month subscription for ad-free content, but that it will also function as a standard podcasting app.
