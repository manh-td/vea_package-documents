💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Newspaper paywalls slowly increasing, but online news is still mostly free](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/risj-review/newspaper-paywalls-slowly-increasing-online-news-still-mostly-free?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

A new study by Reuters Institute challenges the fears about paywalls limiting access to online news. They found that more than two-thirds of leading newspapers in Europe and the US are operating a paywall of some description, but hard paywalls that completely restrict access are rare.

The [full study](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2019-05/Paymodels_for_Online_News_FINAL.pdf?ref=ghost.org) surveyed media in 7 countries and goes into detail about the average costs for paywalled news. Many charts - lots of data!

### [How to engage with your audience - lessons from a failed subscription newsletter](https://www.rjionline.org/stories/short-takes-lessons-from-a-failed-newsletter-in-how-to-engage-with-your-aud?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Co-workers at [Greeley Tribune](https://www.greeleytribune.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter), Colorado were tasked to launch and grow a paid newsletter called The Playbook, covering rural prep sports in Northern Colorado. It failed to meet expectations after 5 months, so it was canned. Here’s what they learnt in the process!

### [What gets readers hooked onto your writing? [Podcast]](https://www.journalism.co.uk/podcast/what-gets-readers-hooked-onto-your-writing/s399/a738303/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“In an ideal world, every piece of journalistic writing would be compelling, sharp and immersive. But in reality, it is all too easy to churn out templated stories that get hardly read past the first paragraph.”

### [The Times continues keeps growing digital-only subscribers (and ad revenue)](https://edition.cnn.com/2019/05/08/media/new-york-times-quarter-one-earnings-2019/index.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

First quarter earnings for the NYT were posted this week and they exceeded expectations with a net income of $30.2 million,  223,000 new digital subscriptions and 19% growth in digital advertising revenue.

### [Find out what readers want first – then turn it into a subscription product!](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/05/the-new-york-times-launches-its-evidence-driven-non-judgy-parenting-vertical-with-an-eye-toward-making-it-a-subscription-product/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Other than record first quarter growth, the New York Times are also expanding into other verticals as a way to test the waters and find out what their audience want, before launching new subscription products. They did this with cooking, next up: parenting
