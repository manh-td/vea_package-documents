### [Inside the box: A look at news, niche, and inspired subscription strategies](https://digitalcontentnext.org/blog/2019/06/13/thinking-inside-the-box-a-look-at-news-niche-and-inspired-subscription-strategies/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [Subscription media: The benefit of making more of your content available for free](https://digiday.com/media/how-sports-ott-platform-dazn-works-out-what-to-give-away-for-free/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Subscription sports broadcaster [Dazn](https://watch.dazn.com/en-US/sports/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) charges a monthly fee for viewers to access live, on-demand sports. But with lofty goals to expand the business, they’ve recently started experimenting with making more content free to access in order to acquire new subscribers.

### [Anyone with more than 30,000 social media followers is now considered a celebrity in the UK](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2019/07/02/anyone-30000-social-media-followers-considered-celebrity-advertising/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [Superhuman says it will disable email read receipts by default after privacy controversy](https://www.theverge.com/2019/7/3/20681655/superhuman-email-app-spying-controversy-policy-change-read-receipts?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Email startup [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/?ref=ghost.org) found itself caught up in controversy about how it uses tracking pixels, which allows users to see when and *where* people open their emails without consent. After some contemplation, the CEO Rahul Vohra wrote a thoughtful public apology, and announced that Superhuman will stop tracking location and turn off read receipts by default.
