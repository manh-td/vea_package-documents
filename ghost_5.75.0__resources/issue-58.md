💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Converting news readers into paying subscribers: a primer for publishers](https://www.twipemobile.com/converting-news-readers-into-paying-subscribers-primer-publishers/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*“With 52% of publishers focusing on subscriptions as their main revenue source this year, the need to better convert subscribers is continuing to grow.”*

Twipe break down some of the top learnings from recent research about subscription media. According to the stats, readers are more heavily persuaded by what they’ll gain from a subscription, rather than what they’d lose if they don’t subscribe.

### [Mozilla teases $5-per-month ad-free news subscription](https://www.theverge.com/2019/7/5/20683059/mozilla-news-subscription-service-ad-free-scroll-price?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Mozilla has started teasing an ad-free news subscription service, which, for $5 per month, would offer ad-free browsing, audio readouts, and cross-platform syncing of news articles from a number of websites.”

### [With sports, local newspapers try 'unbundling' the subscription](https://digiday.com/media/sports-local-newspapers-try-unbundling-subscription/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“The rookie campaigns of many newspapers’ digital sports subscriptions are over. Now comes the work of developing the standalone subscription products into long-lasting sources of revenue.”

### [How Nordic giant Schibsted 'saves' unsubscribers](https://digiday.com/media/nordic-giant-schibsted-saves-unsubscribers/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Here’s another example of a publisher employing tactics that are well known in the SaaS space. Norwegian outlet Shibsted has been busy working on user journeys and implementing a churn-stopping user management feature that allows subscribers to change or pause their memberships. Here’s [another article from INMA](https://www.inma.org/blogs/marketing/post.cfm/schibsted-reduces-churn-by-helping-readers-visualise-a-subscription-s-value-proposition?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) that takes a deep dive on some of the tactics they’re using.

### [A landscape study of local news models across America](https://shorensteincenter.org/landscape-local-news-models/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

[Heidi Legg](https://twitter.com/heidilegg?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) published a new Shorestein Center paper that takes a look at digital subscriptions and emerging business models for both non-profit and for-profit organisations. See also: an [overview](https://shorensteincenter.org/landscape-local-news-models-nonprofit-models/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) of 21 examples of news outlets who are building subscription businesses.

--------------------

### [Where should analytics live within a media organisation?](https://www.inma.org/blogs/value-content/post.cfm/where-should-analytics-live-within-a-media-organisation?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

When news brands debate where to house data analytics functions within an organisation, they must put the business case first and avoid keeping data in a silo. Here are five tips to do it right.

### [Is “news on the internet” the same as “news on your phone”?](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/07/is-news-on-the-internet-the-same-as-news-on-your-phone-heres-how-pew-asks/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

How do we calculate the true trends of people reading the news on the internet? Is reading a story on an app on on social media the same as reading a story online? The [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) has been perfecting how they survey people about their reading habits for years - [Elisa Shearer](https://twitter.com/elisashearer?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) shares some insights from the inside.

### [Working across disciplines: A manifesto for happy newsrooms](https://niemanreports.org/articles/working-across-disciplines-a-manifesto-for-happy-newsrooms/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

This piece from NiemanReports argues interdisciplinary teams are essential in the modern newsroom. Publishing in 2019 and beyond opens the doors for new approaches and new skills in newsrooms: coding, designing and data science to name a few. But to make this work, publishers need to adopt a fresh perspective!
