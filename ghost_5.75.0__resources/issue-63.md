### [Tighten up that paywall! (And some other lessons from a study of 500 newspaper publishers)](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/08/tighten-up-that-paywall-and-some-other-lessons-from-a-study-of-500-newspaper-publishers/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Since The New York Times introduced a paywall in 2011, it has become popular with a majority of newspapers and a growing business model for independent publishers across the world.

Research into a wide range of these publications suggests that pay model strategies with higher “stop rates” are doing better - which means giving out less content for free before asking readers to pay. Having a simple checkout process and understanding your readers is also important. Find out more in this NiemanLab piece.

### [PodPass wants to build the identity layer for podcasting (before some big tech company does it first)](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/08/podpass-wants-to-build-the-identity-layer-for-podcasting-before-some-big-tech-company-does-it-first/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

As podcasting grows, and paid podcasting becomes a hot topic - there’s still an issue which needs to be addressed: Podcast listeners use several apps and platforms - so can podcast subscriptions be integrated into an open ecosystem? The PodPass proposal hopes to solve s0me of these issues with an open protocol that provides a better experience for listeners, and more control for podcasters.

### [Could Facebook license publishers’ content?](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/08/facebook-is-reportedly-interested-in-licensing-publishers-content-for-its-news-tab-this-fall/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

It has been reported that Facebook is in talks with multiple large publishers to offer them a licensing fee of $3 million to allow them to show content in the Facebook “News” tab.

### [The Financial Times story playbook](https://gijn.org/2019/08/06/how-the-ft-improved-storytelling-with-templates-that-you-can-use-too/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The FT approached a problem shared by many publishers: How do we share content clearly and consistently at scale?To explore this, they have built a playbook of standardised and proven templates that can be used directly in a CMS by their entire newsroom. They [shared the end result on GitHub](https://github.com/ft-interactive/story-playbook?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) so that others can learn from their processes, see their reference materials and get tips on how to create their own templates.
