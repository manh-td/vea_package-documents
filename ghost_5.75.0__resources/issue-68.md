✍️ Modern journalism
--------------------

### [After a $2.6M crowdfunding campaign, The Correspondent will have just one full-time US journalist](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/09/after-2-6m-crowdfunding-campaign-the-correspondent-will-have-just-one-full-time-journalist-in-the-u-s/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The Correspondent is hiring a total of five full-time journalists who will be based in Italy, Nigeria, India, London and the U.S.

> *“The U.S.-based journalist is* [*Eric Holthaus*](https://twitter.com/EricHolthaus?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)*, who will be reporting on climate change from Minnesota, though his stories won’t focus on the U.S.: “I’ll be telling stories that are collaborative, constructive, and transnational”.*

### [“The Daily” hits one billion downloads!](https://www.nytco.com/press/the-daily-hits-one-billion-downloads/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*The New York Times*’ *The Daily* podcast has reached a milestone of 1 billion downloads since its launch in February 2017. An impressive achievement for the publisher, alongside having [4.7 million total subscriptions](https://investors.nytco.com/investors/investor-news/investor-news-details/2019/The-New-York-Times-Company-Reports-2019-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx?ref=ghost.org). The outlet credits the success of their podcast growth to the work and creativity of the TimesAudio team.

### [What exactly is a newspaper edition in today's digital age?](https://www.twipemobile.com/what-exactly-newspaper-edition-today-digital-age/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Dive into defining the edition concept in a digital world with Twipe and take a look at replicating print, digital native editions, newsletters and other emerging formats that are replacing printed newspaper editions.

### [As regulators investigate, Google is now funding the creation of local news sites in the US and the UK](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/09/as-regulators-investigate-google-is-now-funding-the-creation-of-local-news-sites-in-the-us-and-the-uk/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Google has now committed “millions” of dollars/pounds in support of the creation of three local news sites in two different countries by major media companies (that already have local news sites).”

### [BBC experiments with negative news filters to help readers with anxieties](https://www.journalism.co.uk/news/bbc-experiments-with-flitering-out-negative-news-from-homepage/s2/a744681/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*“As one third of audience switches off from news, the public broadcaster is testing a tool that would allow readers to blur out stories that may impact their mental health.”*The idea is not to filter or remove news, but to give users the control.

### [Washington Post builds ad network for publishers to take on Big Tech](https://www.axios.com/washington-post-zeus-prime-advertising-8f356787-5f66-4fc2-9d34-a62516d4762d.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)
