### [Graphtreon: Earnings, graphs and rankings of thousands of independent creators](https://graphtreon.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [How publishers re-engage dormant email subscribers](https://digiday.com/media/publishers-reengage-dormant-email-subscribers/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Publishers who send email newsletters have to find ways to improve open rates and click-through rates. Nothing hurts these metrics more than having a huge list of disengaged subscribers - so what methods can you use to either re-engage or remove churned email readers?

Find out how popular newsletters such as Morning Brew are managing their email lists in this Digiday article.

### [The News Revenue Hub is launching a pilot project to help news orgs increase their readers’ loyalty](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/10/the-news-revenue-hub-is-launching-a-pilot-project-to-help-news-orgs-increase-their-readers-loyalty/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*"You can turn loyal readers into members and subscribers. But first, you have to turn occasional readers into loyal readers."*

This article explains how the well-established [conversion funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_funnel?ref=ghost.org) relates to publishers, and what they should be doing to optimise their funnel as membership and subscription models start to take the place of advertising.

### [Unexpected ways publishers engage readers](https://www.twipemobile.com/publishers-seeing-success-side-platforms/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Apps, newsletters and social media are some of the most obvious building blocks of a digital strategy in publishing - but there's many other innovative ways to engage with your audience. This article explores three less obvious trends: Building communities, using calendars to deliver news and forming reader habits with WhatsApp groups.

### [The October edition of your monthly guide to sending better newsletters is here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pySiYQ_5OMsj1U4r5XXLPru0NtiVg2FVaO5INBlNffk/view?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The latest tips, tools and advice about building an email newsletter from Dan at *Not a Newsletter* has arrived. This monthly Google Doc curation is one of our top reads each month. We really enjoyed the newsletter spotlight in this issue, with real insights from an independent newsletter writer, [Pit Gottschalk](http://newsletter.pitgottschalk.de/?ref=ghost.org), who has over 12k subscribers.

### [“Publishers are going to live or die based on their relationship with readers”: How Quartz is rethinking its membership offerings](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/10/publishers-are-going-to-live-or-die-based-on-their-relationship-with-readers-how-quartz-is-rethinking-its-membership-offerings/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

[Quartz](https://qz.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) have announced major leadership changes and are rethinking their membership strategy with a new subscription that is “similar to an Audible.com subscription, where you’re getting access to this huge library of journalism, than it is to a daily news subscription.”

### [The Information will launch Ticker, a tech news app aimed at consumers](https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/17/the-information-ticker/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Typically aimed at professionals, the membership news site offers $399 annual subscriptions for investors, entrepreneurs and tech industry executives. Their new app *Ticker* will be aimed towards consumers, with a much lower price point of $29 per year.
