### [Paths to subscription: Why recent subscribers chose to pay for news](https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/publications/reports/survey-research/paths-to-subscription/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Price elasticity tests yield revenue opportunities for publishers](https://www.inma.org/blogs/value-content/post.cfm/price-elasticity-tests-yield-revenue-opportunities-for-publishers?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Mather Economics ran some pricing tests with several publishers to measure the elasticity of digital-only subscriber price. They found that the ideal renewal price is often higher than expected, and digital-only subscribers are less sensitive to price changes than print subscribers.

### [What it takes to shift a news organisation to reader revenue](https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/reader-revenue/what-it-takes-to-shift-a-news-organization-to-reader-revenue/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*“Launching a paywall is easy. Pivoting a whole business from an advertising-centric mindset to one focused on reader revenue is not.”*

API investigate the ways in which the news industry needs to develop the skills and technology to sustain membership and subscription programs.

### [Subscriber-only newsletters aim to build local news loyalty](https://localnewsinitiative.northwestern.edu/posts/2019/10/23/newsletters-subscriber-only/index.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“We’ve seen data from Google that shows that email newsletters are the single most effective way to get people to decide to subscribe”

### [It’s time to get personal with consumers, or get lost](https://www.rjionline.org/stories/its-time-to-get-personal-with-consumers-or-get-lost?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“There is a limit to how many publications a consumer will be willing to pay for, and if you want to be one of them, you’ll need to:

1. give people content so indispensable they cannot live without, or
2. make them feel connected to something so deeply that they want help keep it alive.”

### [What hope for slow journalism?](https://medium.com/global-editors-network/what-hope-for-slow-journalism-4c733382cede?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Global Editors Network catch up with James Harding and the founding team of [Tortoise](https://www.tortoisemedia.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) to discuss the opportunities and challenges of slow journalism.

### [Glamour Editor in Chief on transforming into a digital-first brand [Podcast]](https://voices.media/glamour-editor-chief-deborah-joseph-transforming-digital-first-brand/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Glamour UK’s Editor in Chief Deborah Joseph talks Media Voices about the transformation of the publication from print-first to digital-first.

### [What if scale breaks community? Rebooting audience engagement when journalism is under fire](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/our-research/what-if-scale-breaks-community-rebooting-audience-engagement-when-journalism-under?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

The latest report from Reuters Institute investigates how digital media navigates audience engagement while keeping up to date with developments in technology, mobile and platform media.

### [Starting a company to build out a new model for local news](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/10/why-im-starting-a-company-to-build-out-a-new-model-for-local-news-in-the-2020s/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

NiemanLab author [Ken Doctor](https://www.niemanlab.org/author/kdoctor/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) has announced a new company called [Lookout](https://lookout.co/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) and a new model for local news, which will launch next year.
