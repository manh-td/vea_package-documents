-----------------

### [How local news organisations can fight for their slice of the subscription pie](https://www.twipemobile.com/local-news-organisations-fight-slice-subscription-pie/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Subscription businesses have been growing for years - but largely in the tech industry. This article summarises a study from [News Media Alliance](https://www.newsmediaalliance.org/release-new-study-reveals-connection-entertainment-news-media-subscriptions/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) about news media subscriptions and how publishers can get their share of the pie.

### [Tortoise claims nearly 20,000 members as it eyes move into podcasting](https://www.pressgazette.co.uk/tortoise-media-claims-20000-members-eyes-move-into-podcasting-james-harding/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Last year James Harding left his role at the BBC to launch his own slow news publishing outlet. More than half a million pounds in funding later, [Tortoise Media](https://www.tortoisemedia.com/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) has 20,000 members within the first six months - here’s what they plan to do next.

### [Focusing on this metric let a newspaper increase reader loyalty (and subscribers too)](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/11/in-germany-focusing-on-this-metric-let-a-newspaper-increase-reader-loyalty-and-subscribers-too/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

German publisher [DuMont](https://www.dumont.de/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) used to rely on basic web analytics, until they decided these metrics were no longer good enough. Instead they started measuring priority and loyal visitors - correlating this to the likelihood to buy or renew subscriptions. Once these visitors are identified, the publisher sends targeted newsletters, which has resulted in subscription growth.

### [Facebook’s intentions may not be pure, but its money is real: How publishers made the most of its membership accelerator](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/11/facebooks-intentions-may-not-be-pure-but-its-money-is-real-how-publishers-made-the-most-of-its-membership-accelerator/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

✍️ Modern journalism
--------------------

### [“Moments of Next”: Nielsen identifies exactly when readers are most engaged, and how publishers can benefit from it](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/moments-of-next-nielsen-study-identifies-exactly-when-readers-are-most-engaged-and-how-publishers-can-benefit-from-it/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*“What if you could scientifically pinpoint the exact moment people’s attention is at its peak? What if you could identify the very instant they’re ready and willing to see something new?”*

This study found that people prefer to see a feed at the end of your content, and were 20% more attentive than when no further articles were presented at the bottom of the page. Food for thought!

### [Survival tips for nonprofit newsrooms: Fundraising, membership, and sustainable models](https://gijn.org/2019/11/11/survival-tips-for-nonprofit-newsrooms-fundraising-membership-and-sustainable-models/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Revenue options for nonprofit newsrooms now include everything from story sales and membership to crowdfunding and even cryptocurrency tokens.”

### [Journalists-turned-entrepreneurs on how they built their businesses](https://niemanreports.org/articles/journalists-turned-entrepreneurs-on-how-they-built-their-businesses/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

More and more journalists are venturing into media entrepreneurship - learn more about how they did it.

### [Why journalists shouldn’t be afraid of metrics](https://medium.com/we-are-the-european-journalism-centre/why-journalists-shouldnt-be-afraid-of-metrics-1402a24e780b?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [[Free Course] Diversify and grow the ways you earn revenue](https://betternews.org/topic/table-stake-diversify-revenue/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)
