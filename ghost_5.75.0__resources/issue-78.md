💯 Top picks
-----------

### [What 1000 true fans means for today’s journalism startups](https://phillipadsmith.com/2019/05/what-1000-true-fans-means-for-todays-journalism-startups.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

*****“You don’t need a million casual readers, just 1000 committed ones”.***** Phillip Smith wrote this fascinating essay about the concept of getting 1000 true fans for startups in publishing, making one key point: you don’t need to chase a daunting goal, it’s entirely possible to build a successful business around a more attainable number of “true fans”.

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Turn passive readers into engaged members with these tips](https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/reader-revenue/how-to-retain-subscribers-by-keeping-their-engagement-levels-high-over-time/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Onboarding campaigns, special rewards and extra benefits are some of the key things you can do to increase engagement *and* retention. Find out more from this piece from the [Reader Revenue Toolkit](https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/reader-revenue/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter).

### [How TechCrunch uses product perks to drive paid subscriptions](https://digiday.com/media/techcrunch-uses-product-perks-drive-paid-subscriptions/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Speaking of extra benefits – TechCrunch has begun offering extra perks to annual members of Extra Crunch, such as two free months of Aircall, 6 free months with Zendesk and $1000 dollars of AWS credits.

### [How news publishers are chasing international subscriber growth](https://digiday.com/media/how-news-publishers-are-chasing-international-subscriber-growth/?utm_campaign=2ce4e6eff5-dailylabemail3&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Daily%20Lab%20email%20list&utm_term=0_d68264fd5e-2ce4e6eff5-396331633)

“For news publishers chasing digital subscriber growth, international readers are playing an important and growing role.”

### [Why it’s so hard to scale paid podcast subscriptions](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/why-its-so-hard-to-scale-paid-podcast-subscriptions/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

This piece from WNIP by Simon Ownes explores why it’s hard to monetise with subscriptions for content that isn’t text based – with Podcasters and YouTubers relying on ads, or selling to a major streaming service. Fragmented podcast listening apps make this even more difficult by adding increased friction.

✍️ Modern journalism
--------------------

### [How we built a system to let our teams run their own user research projects](https://medium.com/whereby-us/how-we-built-a-system-to-let-our-teams-run-their-own-user-research-projects-12cd7cd81642?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

WhereBy.Us run five local media brands with a small team and minimal resources, so they put together documentation and ran user research training to empower their small teams. This article explains how and why they did it – plus they’ve shared a [full worksheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17LDpW5roMAw2GMRsSERmzwcA5-XZ9vScolBDKXVqOWA/edit?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) so you can follow in their footsteps.

### [Journalism’s Dunbar number: Audience scales, community does not](https://localnewslab.org/2019/03/04/journalisms-dunbar-number-audience-scales-community-does-not/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Evolutionary science came up with a theory that the limit of close social relationships a human can maintain is 150. This article explores the notion that there’s a similar number in media for building communities, and to extend this number, you’ll need more than a marketing campaign.
