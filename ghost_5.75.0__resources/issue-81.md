As always, we'll be sharing the latest and greatest ideas, inspiration and stories for independent publishing every Sunday. To kick things off, we've got lots of interesting hot takes on preparing your publishing strategy for the year ahead.

💯 2020 forecasts
----------------

### [NiemanLab predictions for journalism in 2020](https://www.niemanlab.org/collection/predictions-2020/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

At the end of each year, NiemanLab ask people in journalism and digital media to share what they think is coming in the following 12 months. Here’s the roundup of the latest predictions, including some of our favourites: “[Respect the non-paying audience](https://www.niemanlab.org/2020/01/respect-the-non-paying-audience/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)” & “[A return to blogs (finally? sort of?)](https://www.niemanlab.org/2020/01/a-return-to-blogs-finally-sort-of/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)”

### [[Podcast] What 2020 holds for the publishing industry, from the streaming wars to the end of the cookie](https://www.stitcher.com/podcast/the-digiday-podcast/e/66185031?autoplay=true&utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Digiday’s reporters weigh in on the year ahead and discuss the streaming wars, a future that goes “beyond the cookie” and how publishers’ revenue streams may change (29 minute listen).

### [Here are 10 themes for news in 2020 from the NYT](https://open.nytimes.com/looking-forward-to-2020-here-are-10-themes-for-news-166d84125172?gi=fea6f8c3365b&utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“The New York Times Product and Design Discovery team took a human-centered design research process to understand how people use technology and what that might mean for news.”

### [Publishers will arm up for the churn war](https://digiday.com/media/2020-publishers-will-arm-churn-war/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

2019 saw lots publishers putting up paywalls and introducing subscriptions. This growth in recurring reader revenue against a backdrop of a failing advertising market has been the beacon of hope of the past 12 months. But as in any business with recurring revenue will attest, the next part of the journey will be battling with churn! Digiday discusses how the more mature publications will be approaching this in 2020.

💸 Business models
-----------------

### [Publishers are joining the race for reader revenues, but there’s no silver bullet](https://voices.media/publishers-joining-race-reader-revenues-theres-no-silver-bullet/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

"Publishers large and small are joining earlier adopters with their own reader revenues plays. But with subscription fatigue threatening, quality content and smart reader retention strategies will be necessary for success”.

### [Newsday marries newsletters, audience data to increase subscriptions, engagement](https://www.inma.org/blogs/value-content/post.cfm/newsday-marries-newsletters-audience-data-to-increase-subscriptions-engagement?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Senior Editor at Newsday, Anthony Bottan, shares how they have driven subscriptions and increased engagement through newsletters by using audience data to identify high-interest topics among users with impressive results.

### [200k subscribers: How one of Poland’s leading newspapers did it](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/from-170k-to-200k-subscribers-in-2019-how-gazeta-wyborcza-one-of-polands-leading-newspapers-did-it/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

[Gazeta Wyborcza](https://wyborcza.pl/0,0.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter), one of Poland’s leading newspapers now has over 200,000 subscribers, ranking 23rd in the [global digital subscriptions snapshot](https://www.fipp.com/news/insightnews/global-digital-subscriptions-snapshot-november-2019?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter). They share the attributes that helped them to achieve this success here!

### [The changing business model for publishers, in five charts](https://digiday.com/media/digiday-research-changing-business-model-publishers-five-charts/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“For publishers, the biggest priorities heading into 2020 are how to grow revenue they can control: Both through direct-sold ads and subscriptions.”

[Download the end of year charts in a zip file.](http://digiday.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/5-Charts-End-of-Year.zip?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [How to win the loyalty of new subscribers through great onboarding](https://www.americanpressinstitute.org/reader-revenue/how-to-win-the-loyalty-of-new-subscribers-through-great-onboarding/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Onboarding campaigns are well versed in the SaaS industry, and are becoming increasingly popular for subscription publishers to improve engagement with new paid subscribers. Check out this article to find out how real publishers are implementing onboarding in their subscription flows.
