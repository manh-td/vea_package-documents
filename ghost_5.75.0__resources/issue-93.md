### [More creators than ever are sharing their work on Patreon](https://blog.patreon.com/covid-19-creative-economy-patreon-data-science/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [What publishers can learn from The Independent’s growth story](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/profitable-since-going-all-digital-what-publishers-can-learn-from-the-independents-growth-story/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

### [Despite relaxing paywalls, publishers like Bloomberg, WSJ and The Atlantic see subscription spikes](https://digiday.com/media/despite-relaxing-paywalls-publishers-like-bloomberg-wsj-atlantic-see-subscription-spikes/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)
