




#94 — Subscription economy has grown by 321%








































In this weeks roundup of indie publishing news we hear of more publishers gaining more subscribers than ever, and enjoy a really hot take on Twitter about the need to build a new decentralised media, staffed by citizen journalists.

💯 Top picks
-----------

### ["Subscription Economy has grown its revenue by 321%": Highlights for publishers from 'The Forever Transaction'](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/subscription-economy-has-grown-its-revenue-by-321-highlights-for-publishers-from-the-forever-transaction/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Zuora’s Q4 2018 Subscription Index report states, “an average company in the Subscription Economy has grown its revenue by 321% since the launch of the index in January 2012, a compound annual growth rate of 18.1%.”

💸 Business models
-----------------

### ['Opening the paywall is not an option': Schibsted sees subscriptions mini-boom](https://digiday.com/media/opening-the-paywall-is-not-an-option-schibsted-sees-subscriptions-mini-boom/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Nordic publisher Schibsted have sold twice as many daily subscriptions to its multiple news brands in Sweden and Norway in the past few weeks. But with monthly, three-monthly and annual subscriptions - it's hard to know if these new readers will stick around, or churn out when times are less uncertain.

### [From $100M in debt to 98% growth in digital revenues: Lessons from Trusted Media Brands](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/from-100m-in-debt-to-98-growth-in-digital-revenues-lessons-for-publishers-from-trusted-media-brands-remarkable-turnaround-to-profitability/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Trusted Media Brands, the publisher of Reader’s Digest, has gone from being bankrupt twice (2009 and 2013) and struggling with [100M in debt](https://www.cnbc.com/2020/03/02/how-readers-digest-transformed-into-a-digital-multimedia-brand.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter), to becoming profitable again. Its digital ad revenues are now growing, and the company is diversifying into direct-to-consumer products and membership programs.”

### [Local news outlets drop paywalls but gain digital subscribers anyway](https://localnewsinitiative.northwestern.edu/posts/2020/03/25/digital-subscriptions-virus/index.html?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

“Even though many local news organisations have moved their COVID-19 stories outside their paywalls, several are seeing encouraging boosts in their digital subscriptions during the crisis.”

### [Podcasts drive loyalty and engagement between journalists and listeners](https://whatsnewinpublishing.com/how-the-telegraph-uses-podcasts-to-drive-loyalty-and-engagement-between-journalists-and-listeners/?utm_campaign=Publisher%20Weekly&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter)

Publishers like The Telegraph are finding ways to tell stories differently using audio, to reach new audiences and build loyalty.
