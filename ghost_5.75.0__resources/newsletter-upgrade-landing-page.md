




Newsletter landing pages: How to get more paid subscribers









































To recap part one of this paid-for newsletter landing page series, the journey a user takes to convert into a paying subscriber is usually:

1. Email sign up to your free or limited-trial newsletter
2. You deliver what you say while upselling the premium offering
3. Them upgrading with a subscription payment

If you missed part one, catch up on what makes a good [newsletter landing page](https://ghost.org/resources/newsletter-signup-landing-page-tips/).

Part two will move on to:

💳 The newsletter upgrade landing page
-------------------------------------

Your newsletter **upgrade landing page** can be the difference between a free newsletter subscriber and an increase in ARR for your business.

A lot of the components are the same as the **sign up landing page** except we swap out the sign up form for pricing table, while handpicking the absolute best social proof and time-saving reminders.

Here are 7 tips to help you create a high-conversion newsletter upgrade landing page:

### 1. Remind them of the compounding value

One of the biggest attractions to starting a paid-for newsletter is the compounding value of your efforts over time. Let's remind them how it benefits them too.

**✅ "Access to the complete archive of over 350 issues"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/benedicts-newsletter-1.jpeg)

The [Benedict's Newsletter](https://www.ben-evans.com/newsletter?ref=ghost.org) upgrade landing page

**✅ "Full access to our archive of 1021 posts"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/the-browser-pricing.jpeg)

[The Browser](https://thebrowser.com/pricing/?ref=ghost.org)'s newsletter upgrade page

**✅ "Access to our data library"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/tp-upgrade.jpeg)

[The Plug](https://tpinsights.com/membership/?ref=ghost.org)

### 2. Repeat on time-saving

Paying is justified when further time is saved.

**✅ "Save 2,000+ Hours of Market Research"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/trends-vc-upgrade.jpeg)

The [Trends Pro](https://trendsvc.spiffy.co/checkout/trends-pro/?ref=ghost.org) upgrade page explains how much time paid-subscribers can save

### 3. Add an "opinion leader" testimonial

Unlike part one where the testimonials should highlight what the newsletter delivers, we now want to spotlight only the most famous person in our niche.

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/2PM-testimonial.jpeg)

An upgrade page testimonial on [The Plug](https://tpinsights.com/membership/?ref=ghost.org)

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/ben-testimonials.jpeg)

[Benedict's Newsletter](https://www.ben-evans.com/newsletter?ref=ghost.org) upgrade testimonials from tech influencers

### 4. Show paid-for benefits vs free limits

Your pricing table should clearly position your paid-for offering against the free they are currently consuming.

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/femstreet.jpeg)

[Femstreet](https://femstreet.substack.com/about?ref=ghost.org)'s pricing table

### 5. Offer annual pricing

It's easier for a lot of people and great for cash flow.

**✅ "1 quick payment instead of 12"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/rediverge-pricing.jpeg)

[Rediverge](https://rediverge.com/membership/?ref=ghost.org)

**✅ "A saving of $12 a year"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/the-browser-pricing-1.jpeg)

[The Browser](https://thebrowser.com/pricing/?ref=ghost.org)

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/ben-pricing.jpeg)

[Benedict's Newsletter](https://www.ben-evans.com/newsletter?ref=ghost.org)

### 6. FAQs can prevent a closed browser tab

Use your newsletter upgrade landing page footer area for pre-sale support.

If your visitor has not committed by the end of the scroll, they still have doubts. Insert your FAQs, along with your support details:

✅ **Curated to most common questions related to membership:**  
✅ **Add an email address:**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/rediverge-faq.jpeg)

[Rediverge](https://rediverge.com/membership/?ref=ghost.org)

### 7. Consider Community

And the final tip, one to seriously consider, is offering access to a community of like-minded folk has huge value to people.

**✅ "Reports + Community Access"**  
**✅ Join 1000+ Successful Entrepreneurs"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/trends-vc-upgrade-1.jpeg)

[Trends Pro](https://trendsvc.spiffy.co/checkout/trends-pro/?ref=ghost.org) 

**✅ "Our members-only dinners and roundtables are intimate, welcoming, and worthwhile"**

![](https://ghost.org/resources/content/images/2021/06/2PM-pricing.jpeg)

[The Plug](https://tpinsights.com/membership/?ref=ghost.org)

The Internet was built to bring people together to access the globe.

You have created a wonderful corner of the Internet where like-minded people can potentially interact and even do business.

There is **huge value** in this and it's worth a shot!
