





🐛 How to debug your Ghost theme once and for all









































---


Welcome to our second issue of the **Build with Ghost** newsletter! It’s for creators of all levels who are interested in making Ghost their own. We’ll keep you up to date with changes in Ghost, offer tips and tricks for building with Ghost, and highlight exciting Ghost sites in the tech space. If you missed our inaugural issue, [catch up here](https://ghost.org/tutorials/build-ghost-themes-more-quickly-with-our-new-vs-code-extension/).

🏋️**Power-user tip:** Quickly bring up the card menu by pressing forward slash `/` in the editor.

Bugs hate this one trick. (Hint: It’s the `log` helper.)
--------------------------------------------------------

We just published a new tutorial that’s essential for doing more with your Ghost theme. It shows you how to use Ghost’s [`log` helper](https://ghost.org/docs/themes/helpers/log/?ref=ghost.org) to gain a backstage pass to your theme and its data. With the `log` helper, you can know which data is available no matter where you are in the theme, and, importantly, it can help you figure out why data isn’t rendering as expected.

[How to debug your Ghost theme with the log helperBoost your bug-squashing ability, amplify your creative possibilities, and unlock a whole new perspective for custom theme development by learning how to use Ghost’s log helper![](https://ghost.org/tutorials/content/images/size/w256h256/2022/05/ghost-logo-orb.png)TutorialsTeam Ghost![](https://ghost.org/tutorials/content/images/2023/01/Debug.jpg)](https://ghost.org/tutorials/debug/)

The tutorial follows Jamie as they add some features to their site, *Cats in Art*. This (fictional) site provides commentary on artworks that contain cats and proves that before there were cat pictures on the internet, there were cat paintings on canvas, and before that, cat drawings on caves. Images of cats are universal 🐈

In the tutorial, you’ll learn everything you need to know about using the `log` helper, from setting up your developer environment to advanced use cases. The `log` helper will not only speed up your debugging process but also spark your creativity by revealing new possibilities for theme development. It’s a tool worth learning.
