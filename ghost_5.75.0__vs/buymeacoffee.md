Turn your audience into a business, and keep all of your revenue with Ghost
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Buy Me a Coffee is a membership tool that can be used to collect basic payments with social media page and an embeddable widget. Using Buy Me a Coffee, they keep from **5%** of all the revenue you make, and you don’t have any control over what your profile looks like or what features it has.

Ghost is a full-featured publishing platform with native memberships and subscriptions built-in **0%** transaction fees. No matter how large your audience – all the revenue you make is yours to keep. Ghost has more features, so it takes a bit more work to set up - but you own and control the technology, so it’s much more reliable in the long-run.

Build your own brand with Ghost
-------------------------------

Ghost is all about giving you the tools to publish great content, but never locking you into one particular way of doing it. The platform comes with a clean, minimal default theme included, but you can also completely change and customise the front-end of your site to suit your specific needs.

Since Buy Me a Coffee is a closed platform with only basic design settings, you’re confined to changing a few colours. This is fine if you want to build a small social profile — but not so useful if you want to run a publication or have real control over your brand.

**With Ghost:** You choose your domain, you choose your design, you choose your branding, and you choose your links. Each Ghost site is unique, and reflects the personality of its creator. Some examples:

How does this work?
===================

Unlike other membership systems which typically take **10-30%** of your revenue, Ghost takes **no fee** at all and instead connects directly to your own [Stripe account](https://stripe.com/accept-payments/ghost), so you receive the money - not us. You own the software and you own the billing account, so there's no middleman between you and your customers. Stripe takes a ~2.9% processing fee, and all other revenue is yours to keep.

Building a subscription business is challenging in lots of ways. Our focus is on making the billing side as easy as possible - so you can keep your focus on your customers.
